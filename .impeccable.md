## Design Context

### Users

The Cognition Console is used by a mixed internal team: founders and researchers who need full policy and training detail, engineers who need operational evidence and audit trails, and non-technical teammates who need to understand what the system remembered in plain language. Their main jobs are to see what changed, understand why a memory decision happened, inspect whether a memory helped or hurt later work, correct or retire memories, and decide whether a candidate policy is safe to promote.

### Brand Personality

Clear, rigorous, and humane. The interface should create calm confidence and a sense of control. It should make a complex learning system feel inspectable without making it feel simplistic or magical.

### Aesthetic Direction

Match the Agentic Stack desktop product: cool white and light-gray macOS surfaces, compact system typography, thin neutral dividers, lavender selection and focus states, and restrained semantic colors. The interface should feel like a native inspection tool rather than a warm editorial page or a generic analytics dashboard. Use `-apple-system` / SF Pro with SF Mono fallbacks and no remote font dependency. Avoid gradients, glass effects, oversized type, excessive cards, heavy borders, and decorative charts. Dark mode, accessibility, reduced motion, and reliable local operation remain mandatory.

### Design Principles

1. Start with a plain-language answer, then let people reveal provenance, rewards, and raw events progressively.
2. Make trust visible: every memory and policy decision should show its source, status, confidence, and downstream evidence.
3. Use color as a scarce semantic signal for helpful, uncertain, harmful, and blocked states; never rely on color alone.
4. Prefer a readable ledger, timeline, and relationship views over repeated dashboard cards or decorative metrics.
5. Keep consequential actions explicit and reversible; policy promotion remains a human decision and memory deletion should preserve an audit trail.
6. Design mobile layouts as focused inspection views, while desktop layouts expose richer comparison and evidence panes.
